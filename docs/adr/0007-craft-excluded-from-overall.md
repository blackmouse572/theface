# The Overall excludes Craft Ratings

Status: accepted. Date: 2026-09-18

TheFace computes the Overall from Feature and Impression Ratings only. Craft Ratings -
lighting, angle, framing, background, expression authenticity, camera distance - carry a
weight of zero.

## Why

Craft describes the photograph. It does not describe the person. A face-rating product that
lowers a person's Overall because of a bad lamp measures the wrong thing.

TheFace also builds its advice from Craft Ratings. If a Craft Rating also lowered the
Overall, TheFace would penalise the Visitor for a fault that the advice already reports. The
advice would then read as criticism instead of help.

## Consequences

This decision is difficult to reverse. The Tally's 101 counters are defined by the meaning of
the Overall. A change to the composition invalidates every historical counter.

The rule is surprising, so state it wherever the Overall is computed.
